How it works

From your first coin to your first trade

  1. 01

    Get 1,000 coins

    Every new account starts with 1,000 coins. They open packs and sweeten trades, and they never turn into cash.

  2. 02

    Open packs

    Every pack lists its odds before you open it. Your pulls land in your collection the moment it opens.

  3. 03

    Build decks

    Drag cards into a deck. Deck size, the four-copy limit and format legality are checked as you go.

  4. 04

    Trade

    Offer cards or coins to any collector. Both sides stay locked until the swap settles.
